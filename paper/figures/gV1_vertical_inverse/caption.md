**The 1D inverse action-angle map, differentiable end to end.** `actionAngleVerticalInverse` maps $(J,\theta)\to(z,v_z)$ through a momentum-matched canonical torus map. Its construction (turning points, spectral torus sweeps, Gauss-Newton anomaly-map fits, Hermite interpolants in $J$) and its evaluation (a bracketed root find for the anomaly) both run natively in jax, so a single `jax.jit` covers the whole path from potential parameter to $(z, v_z, \Omega)$. The examples use a self-gravitating isothermal disk with $\sigma_0=0.5$ and amp $=1$. (a) Tori at five fixed actions (solid), the tori rebuilt at $\sigma_0+0.1$ (dashed), and the first-order prediction from autodiff, torus$(\sigma_0)+\Delta\sigma\,\partial_\sigma$torus (dots). Near the midplane the disk is harmonic with $\nu^2=4\pi\,$amp, whatever $\sigma$ is, so only the larger tori move (zoom). (b) Forward-mode AD against a 5-point central finite difference along the $J=0.06$ torus (64 angles, including both turning points). Each $\sigma$ finite difference rebuilds the whole family. Errors relative to $\max|{\rm AD}|$ are $\le1.4\times10^{-10}$ for $\partial_\sigma(z,v_z)$, $2\times10^{-11}$ for $\partial_\sigma\Omega$ and $\le2.3\times10^{-12}$ for $\partial_J$ and $\partial_\theta$. The Poisson bracket $\{z,v_z\}$ computed from the AD Jacobian equals 1 to $9\times10^{-16}$, an exact canonicity check that uses no finite differences. (c) The finite difference converges onto AD as $h^4$ until it reaches its own round-off floor, which sets the agreement levels in (b). (d) The disk fitted from 120 noisy stars ($\delta z=\delta v_z=0.01$) on three tori of known action but unknown angle. Levenberg-Marquardt fits $(\sigma,{\rm amp})$ and the 120 angles together, using a Jacobian made entirely by AD ($\partial_\sigma$, $\partial_{\rm amp}$ through the construction and $\partial_{\theta_i}$ through the evaluation). It converges in 10 steps from $(0.7, 0.6)$ to $\sigma=0.460\pm0.021$, amp $=1.037\pm0.030$ ($\chi^2=98.4$ for 118 dof), and the truth lies inside the 95% contour.
